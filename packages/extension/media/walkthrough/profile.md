## Tell the tutor about yourself

The learner profile is sent with every question, so answers fit you. For example:

> Comfortable with Python, new to TypeScript generics. Prefer short explanations and one hint at a time.

Other settings worth a look: the tutor **model** (also in the status bar), **show reasoning**, the **cache lifetime** (1 hour by default; the countdown sits in the status bar), and how much **context** the tutor gets (the ring in the status bar shows how full it is).
